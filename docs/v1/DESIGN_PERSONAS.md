## Persona 1: Reseller (Maria)

### Demographics

- **Age:** 32 years old
- **Location:** Munich, Germany
- **Background:** Former marketing professional, now stay-at-home parent
- **Education:** Bachelor's degree in Business Administration
- **Income:** Looking to supplement household income
- **Family:** Married, 2 children, 1 dog

### Motivation

Maria wants to earn income while contributing to animal welfare. She's passionate about animals and sees MIRA as a way to combine her values with earning potential. She's motivated by:
- Financial independence while staying home with children
- Making a positive impact on animal welfare
- Building a sustainable income stream
- Recognition and achievement through gamification

### Goals

1. **Build a team** of 20+ active resellers within 6 months
2. **Advance ranks** from Bronze to Gold within 3 months
3. **Maximize commission** earnings to €1,000+ per month
4. **See impact** - track how her network contributes to animal welfare
5. **Achieve recognition** through leaderboards and badges

### Pain Points

1. **Unclear progress** - Doesn't know how close she is to next rank
2. **Delayed payouts** - Wants faster access to earnings
3. **Lack of recognition** - Feels invisible when not in top 10
4. **Complex commission structure** - Hard to understand earnings breakdown
5. **Network visibility** - Can't easily see team member activity
6. **Time constraints** - Limited time due to parenting responsibilities

### Tech Literacy

- **Level:** Moderate to High
- **Comfort with:** Mobile apps, social media, online shopping
- **Challenges:** Complex dashboards, too many options
- **Preferences:** Visual data, simple navigation, mobile-first

### Gamification Engagement

- **Level:** High
- **Responds to:** Badges, leaderboards, challenges, streaks
- **Motivated by:** Progress indicators, achievement unlocks, social recognition
- **Engagement pattern:** Daily check-ins, competitive spirit, milestone celebrations

### Key Behaviors

1. **Daily dashboard checks** - Logs in every morning to check earnings and streak
2. **Shares referral codes** - Actively shares via WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram
3. **Tracks team growth** - Monitors network tree weekly
4. **Competes on leaderboard** - Checks position daily, motivated by rankings
5. **Celebrates milestones** - Shares achievements on social media
6. **Uses mobile app** - Primarily accesses via smartphone

### User Story

> "I want to see my progress toward the next rank at a glance, so I know exactly what actions I need to take to advance. When I earn a commission, I want to see it immediately and understand how it was calculated. Most importantly, I want to see the impact my network is making - that's what keeps me motivated."

### Prototype Implications

- **Critical screens:** Dashboard Home, Rank Tracker, Earnings Overview, Network Tree, Leaderboard
- **Key interactions:** Progress visualization, real-time updates, social sharing
- **Mock data needs:** Realistic earnings progression, rank advancement scenarios, network growth

## Persona 2: Customer (Thomas)

### Demographics

- **Age:** 45 years old
- **Location:** Berlin, Germany
- **Background:** Software engineer, pet owner
- **Education:** Master's degree in Computer Science
- **Income:** €65,000/year
- **Family:** Single, 2 cats

### Motivation

Thomas wants quality pet food with a purpose. He values:
- High-quality nutrition for his pets
- Supporting animal welfare causes
- Convenience of subscription delivery
- Transparency about where his money goes

### Goals

1. **Convenient subscription** - Set it and forget it
2. **Visible impact contribution** - See how his purchase helps animals
3. **Quality products** - Trust the brand and ingredients
4. **Easy management** - Pause or modify subscription when needed

### Pain Points

1. **Unclear where money goes** - Wants transparency on donations
2. **No feedback on impact** - Doesn't see the results of his contribution
3. **Subscription management** - Wants easier pause/cancel options
4. **Delivery issues** - Needs better tracking and communication
5. **Price concerns** - Wants to understand value proposition

### Tech Literacy

- **Level:** Moderate
- **Comfort with:** Online shopping, email, basic apps
- **Challenges:** Complex forms, too many steps
- **Preferences:** Simple checkout, clear information, email confirmations

### Gamification Engagement

- **Level:** Medium
- **Appreciates:** Impact counters, milestone celebrations
- **Less interested in:** Competition, leaderboards, badges
- **Engagement pattern:** Occasional check-ins, shares impact stories

### Key Behaviors

1. **Values convenience** - Prefers one-click reordering
2. **Wants transparency** - Reads impact reports when available
3. **Shares impact stories** - Posts on social media about donations
4. **Reviews products** - Checks product details before subscribing
5. **Manages subscriptions** - Adjusts frequency based on pet needs

### User Story

> "I want to buy quality pet food that actually helps animals. When I subscribe, I want to see clearly how much of my purchase goes to animal welfare. I want to receive updates showing the impact - photos of animals being helped, stories from shelters. That's what makes me feel good about my purchase."

### Prototype Implications

- **Critical screens:** Product Catalogue, Product Detail, Checkout Flow, Order Confirmation, Impact Overview
- **Key interactions:** Clear impact messaging, simple checkout, subscription management
- **Mock data needs:** Product details, impact metrics, subscription scenarios

## Persona 3: Admin (Sarah)

### Demographics

- **Age:** 28 years old
- **Location:** Remote (Switzerland)
- **Background:** Platform operations manager
- **Education:** Bachelor's degree in Business Information Systems
- **Experience:** 3 years in platform operations

### Motivation

Sarah wants efficient platform management and excellent support. She's motivated by:
- Quick issue resolution
- Easy configuration management
- Compliance and audit requirements
- Supporting reseller success

### Goals

1. **Quick issue resolution** - Resolve support tickets within 2 hours
2. **Easy configuration** - Update commission rules without developer help
3. **Compliance** - Maintain complete audit trail
4. **Platform health** - Monitor metrics and catch issues early
5. **User support** - Help resellers succeed

### Pain Points

1. **Complex workflows** - Too many clicks to complete tasks
2. **Lack of visibility** - Can't see all relevant user data in one place
3. **Manual processes** - Wants automation for repetitive tasks
4. **Configuration errors** - Needs validation and preview before deploying
5. **Audit trail gaps** - Wants complete history of all actions

### Tech Literacy

- **Level:** High
- **Comfort with:** Complex dashboards, data analysis, configuration tools
- **Challenges:** None significant
- **Preferences:** Keyboard shortcuts, bulk actions, export capabilities

### Key Behaviors

1. **Frequent dashboard checks** - Monitors platform health multiple times daily
2. **Quick access to user data** - Needs search and filter capabilities
3. **Configures rules regularly** - Updates commission rates monthly
4. **Reviews audit logs** - Checks activity logs for compliance
5. **Exports data** - Generates reports for accounting and analysis

### User Story

> "I need to see all platform metrics at a glance and quickly access any user's profile. When I configure commission rules, I need to preview the impact before deploying. I need a complete audit trail of all my actions for compliance. Most importantly, I need to resolve support issues quickly without jumping between multiple screens."

### Prototype Implications

- **Critical screens:** Admin Overview, User Lists, Commission Configuration, Audit Log
- **Key interactions:** Search/filter, bulk actions, configuration preview, quick navigation
- **Mock data needs:** Platform metrics, user lists, configuration history, audit events

## Prototype Scope Decisions

Based on these personas:

### High Priority Screens
- **Reseller:** Dashboard Home, Rank Tracker, Earnings, Network Tree, Leaderboard
- **Customer:** Product Catalogue, Checkout, Order Confirmation, Impact View
- **Admin:** Overview, User Lists, Commission Config, Audit Log

### Medium Priority Screens
- **Reseller:** Impact Overview, Badges, Commission History
- **Customer:** Subscription Management, Order History
- **Admin:** Order Management, System Settings

### Low Priority (Can Simplify)
- **Reseller:** Detailed commission breakdowns, advanced filters
- **Customer:** Product reviews, detailed impact stories
- **Admin:** Advanced analytics, detailed reports

### Key Interactions to Validate
1. **Reseller:** Progress visualization, social sharing, quick earnings view
2. **Customer:** Simple checkout, impact transparency, subscription management
3. **Admin:** Quick user access, configuration preview, audit trail
