## 2. The Challenge We're Solving

### Your Business Needs

You need a platform that serves three distinct user types, each with unique goals and challenges:

#### For Resellers
- **Challenge:** Resellers need clear visibility into their progress, earnings, and impact to stay motivated
- **Pain Point:** Unclear progress toward next rank, complex commission structures, lack of recognition
- **Business Impact:** Low engagement leads to low retention and reduced network growth

#### For Customers
- **Challenge:** Customers want transparency about their impact and easy subscription management
- **Pain Point:** Unclear where their money goes, no feedback on impact, difficult subscription changes
- **Business Impact:** Low trust leads to cart abandonment and subscription cancellations

#### For Administrators
- **Challenge:** Admins need efficient tools to manage the platform and support users
- **Pain Point:** Complex workflows, lack of visibility, manual processes, configuration errors
- **Business Impact:** Slow issue resolution and configuration errors impact platform health

### The Solution Approach

Instead of building the full application first and discovering UX issues later, we've created this prototype to:

1. **Validate user journeys** before development investment
2. **Test navigation flows** to ensure intuitive user experience
3. **Review content and messaging** to ensure clear communication
4. **Gather stakeholder feedback** early in the process
5. **Reduce development risk** by catching issues upfront
